My name is Aliya Haas. I am a Jr. Data Scientist at AbbVie through Information Research Academic Partnerships (IRAP). I am also a Bioinformatics Master's student at Loyola University Chicago. This past summer, I interned at Tempus AI, where I automated bioinformatics pipeline documentation by developing automation scripts and configuring yaml files to improve UI/UX tooling experience on Tempus Lens, an oncological data analysis platform. I also have done many computational biology research projects. I've used coding to analyze differential gene expression in mice heart data and in human heart failure patients as well as to develop machine learning models that elucidate biological processes such as hearing loss and plasma protein regulation. I co-authored a 2025 publication in "Aging Cell" that explored protein kinase BubR1's role in cardiac health and aging.  In my free time, I am learning how to reduce e-waste and improve tech sustainability at the Re-Compute Lab.
